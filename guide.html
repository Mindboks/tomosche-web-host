How to Use Tomosche

📝
Step 1 Register Your Own Schedule First
Important: Before sharing with friends, you must register your own schedule. Go to the "Calendar" page, select a date, and tap "My Schedule". Enter your event details (time, title, etc.) and save. 💡 This prevents friends from booking times you are already busy.
📅
Step 2 Add Friends
Tap the "Add" button in the bottom menu to display your QR code. Have your friend scan it with the LINE app's QR scanner. Once scanned, they will appear in your Friends list.
🔄
Step 3 Share Your Calendar
Go to "Friends" and select a friend. Tap "Share Calendar" to send them a sharing request. Once they accept, both calendars become visible to each other.
⏰
Step 4 Plan Together
Open the "Calendar" and select a date. Tap "With Friend" to send an event request. Select the friend, set a time, and send the request. They will receive a notification to confirm or decline.
🔔
Step 5 Get Notified
When a friend responds to your event request, you will receive a notification in the app. You can also check the status of each event in the Calendar view.
🌐
Step 6 Privacy & Security
Your calendar is shared only with friends you approve. All data is stored securely and you can revoke sharing anytime. Review our Privacy Policy for more details.
